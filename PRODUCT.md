# Typing Force

## Register

product

## Users

Everyday typists who want to measure their typing and practice specific weaknesses. Portfolio visitors should be able to try the tool immediately without an account.

## Product Purpose

A focused typing practice tool with optional challenges, confirmed by Ryan on November 1, 2026. Help users understand their speed, accuracy, and observable typing habits, then take a useful next practice step. Keep measurements transparent and distinguish session observations from psychological interpretation.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, professional, confirmed by Ryan. Preserve the portfolio's direct, evidence-backed voice.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative dashboards, oversized cards, ornamental gradients, intrusive gamification, and unsupported personality claims. These follow the existing portfolio product context and the confirmed focus on practice.

## Design Principles

1. Start practicing immediately.
2. Explain measurements and show their evidence.
3. Turn errors into actionable drills.
4. Keep challenges optional and all levels available.
5. Keep personal practice history on the user's device.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, readable contrast, non-color error cues, and responsive layouts are baseline requirements. Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA. Touch-keyboard sessions are supported but are not directly comparable to physical-keyboard sessions.
